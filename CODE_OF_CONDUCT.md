# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming, professional place to collaborate. Participate in good faith, be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences, give constructive feedback, and focus on the problem rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
